# Data attribution and publication boundary

Retention Lab uses **WSDM 2018 — KKBOX Churn Prediction Challenge**:
[competition](https://www.kaggle.com/c/kkbox-churn-prediction-challenge),
[official rules](https://www.kaggle.com/c/kkbox-churn-prediction-challenge/rules),
and [data acquisition](https://www.kaggle.com/c/kkbox-churn-prediction-challenge/data).
KKBOX supplied the competition data; Kaggle hosts the competition.

Retention Lab is an independent educational project, not affiliated with,
endorsed by or sponsored by KKBOX or Kaggle. Obtain the original CSVs directly
from the authorized source, complying with its access requirements and rules.
They are not distributed by this repository.

## Review and owner authorization — 2026-10-20

The indexed official rules were reviewed on this date. Section 7.A permits
research/educational use subject to the rules; section 7.B restricts sharing
competition data with nonparticipants. Section 8.B addresses public sharing of
own code subject to third-party rights. The direct rules/data pages did not
expose readable body text in the browser; the rules text was obtained through
indexed official-host results. No additional dataset-specific license was verified.

No express prohibition specifically covering this publication of global metrics
and aggregate visualizations was identified in the conditions that could be
verified. The owner explicitly authorized those outputs subject to that review.
**This is not a claim of express permission to publish derivatives**, a legal
opinion, or permission to distribute competition data. Historical documentation
requiring affirmative aggregate clearance describes the earlier, more restrictive
publication hold; this limited authorization supersedes that local hold, not
KKBOX/Kaggle's rules. If incompatible applicable terms are established, stop the
affected publication and resolve them before further release.

## Published versus private

Public v2 outputs are sampled capacity metrics, summary tables and one raster
aggregate visualization. No dataset, subscriber identifier, individual outcome,
score, ranking, serialized model, cumulative every-K array or private run manifest
is exported. Public provenance contains hashes, not the underlying records.
`data/raw/`, `data/processed/` and serialized models remain ignored. Every-K
counts can reveal outcomes through differencing and must remain private.
